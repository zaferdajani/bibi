07 RAP SHEET

Is this an actual poll or vote?

No. One person can buy from many wallets, and a small number of holders can own a large share of supply. Treat the count as a visible expression of interest, not a measurement of public opinion.

Is this legal commentary?

No. It's satire and protest speech about a public figure and his conduct in public office. It isn't a legal finding and it isn't evidence of one.

Is this about Jewish people or Israelis?

No, and anyone using it that way is misusing it. This is about one head of government and accountability for state conduct. Criticism of a politician is not criticism of a people, and hate speech gets no home here.

What's "1948" on the uniform?

It's the inmate number in the artwork — a reference to the year the modern conflict's displacement began, which is the political point the meme is making.

Does $JAIL have utility?

No, and we won't pretend otherwise. There's no staking, no revenue, no product. It's a meme token. Anyone promising you yield on a meme coin is lying.

Is there a team allocation?

Fair launch on pump.fun — same curve, same start, for everyone. Verify supply distribution yourself on Solscan rather than taking our word for it.

Will I make money?

Probably not. Most meme coins go to zero, fast. Only spend what you would be comfortable setting on fire.
